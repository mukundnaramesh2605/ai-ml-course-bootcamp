# Nimbus support and contact

## Support hours
Live chat support runs Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm India Standard Time.
Outside these hours you can still open a ticket and we reply the next
business day. Enterprise customers get 24 by 7 priority support.

## How to reach us
The fastest channel is live chat inside the app. For account or billing
issues that need verification, email support from the address on your account.
We do not provide phone support on the Starter plan.

## Service status
If the app is slow or unreachable, check the status page before opening a
ticket. Planned maintenance is announced there at least 48 hours in advance.
