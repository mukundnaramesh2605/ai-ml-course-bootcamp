# Nimbus billing and refunds

## Refund policy
Nimbus offers a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.
After 14 days, refunds are given only for annual plans and are prorated for
the unused months. To request a refund, open a billing ticket from your
dashboard and we process money back to the original payment method within
five business days.

## Plans and pricing
The Starter plan is free for one project. The Pro plan is billed monthly or
annually, and the annual option saves you two months. Enterprise pricing is
custom and includes a dedicated success manager.

## Updating your payment method
Add or change a card under Settings, Billing. The new card is charged on your
next renewal date. We never store the full card number, only the last four
digits for your reference.
