# Nimbus accounts and login

## Resetting your password
If you cannot sign in, go to the login page and choose "Forgot password".
Enter the email on your account and we send a reset link that stays valid
for 30 minutes. Open the link, pick a new password of at least 10 characters,
and you are signed back in. If the email does not arrive, check spam, then
contact support to recover account access manually.

## Two factor authentication
Nimbus supports two factor authentication using an authenticator app.
Turn it on under Settings, Security. If you lose your device, use one of the
backup codes you saved during setup to get back in.

## Changing the email on your account
You can update your login email under Settings, Profile. A confirmation link
is sent to the new address and the change takes effect once you click it.
